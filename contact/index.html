Common questions

How do I move to a new phone?

The app keeps its data out of phone backups, so a new phone starts empty. Open the original PDF or photo of your pattern again and match the ruler once on the new screen.

Why does it ask me to match the ruler again?

The ruler match belongs to one screen at one display setting. If you change something like display zoom, the saved match is set aside, because it would no longer be the real size.

Why doesn’t it ask for permission to read my photos?

It doesn’t need one. You pick the pattern with your phone’s own photo or file picker, and the app can read only the file you pick.

The screen switches off while I trace.

The app asks your phone to keep the screen on while you trace, and the Menu says whether it did. If a device refuses, the app tells you. For the best light, turn the screen brightness up to the maximum.

Does it need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. It has no account and works without a connection.
