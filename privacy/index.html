Privacy policy

Quilt Lightbox doesn’t collect any data about you. Everything you put in the app stays on your phone or tablet.

Effective 29 October 2026. Applies to the Quilt Lightbox apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, and to this website.

In short

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No internet use. The app has no server and no code that sends anything over the internet.
  • Free: no purchases, no ads, no analytics or tracking. No third-party SDKs for any of these.
  • No AI. Nothing you open is analysed by an online service.
  • You decide what leaves the phone. Only a picture you choose to save or share, and only where you send it.

What the app keeps, and where

The last pattern you worked on (the file you picked, the page, its size, your clean-up settings, seam allowance, finished pieces and colours), your ruler setting and your preferences (theme and language) are kept in one database inside the app’s private storage. The developer never receives them and cannot see them.

The app asks iOS to leave this data out of iCloud and computer backups, and on Android it turns off Google backup and device-to-device transfer. That keeps your data private, but it also means a new or reset phone starts empty. To carry on, open the original PDF or photo again.

Permissions

The app asks for none. It doesn’t ask for your photo library, storage, camera, microphone, location, contacts, notifications or calendar, and the Android app is not given internet access.

To open a pattern you use your phone’s own photo and file pickers. The app can read only the one file you pick (up to 100 MB), and nothing else on the phone.

Pictures and files you save or share

“Save PNG” hands the cleaned picture to your phone’s share or save screen. Where it goes (Files, Photos, AirDrop, email, another app) is your choice, and that service’s own privacy terms apply. The app sends nothing by itself.

Links

Settings has rows that open this privacy policy and the contact page. They open in your browser only when you tap them. The app never loads these pages by itself.

Payments and ads

None. Quilt Lightbox is free, with no purchases, no subscriptions and no ads, so no store billing or advertising library is in the app.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data asks you to confirm, then removes the saved pattern, the ruler setting and your preferences from the device, and empties the app’s temporary folder. Deleting the app does the same. Pictures you saved or shared are yours and stay where you put them.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site is plain HTML and CSS: no scripts, forms, cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

If you contact us

If you email for support, your email address and message are used only to reply to you, and aren’t shared with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. A change that means data leaves your phone in a new way would also be explained in the app first.
