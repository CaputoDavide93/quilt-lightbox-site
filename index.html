Trace quilt patterns at true size

Quilt Lightbox turns your phone or tablet into a lightbox. Open a PDF or a photo of your pattern, match the on-screen ruler to a real one, tell the app how big the pattern really is, then lay fabric on the screen and trace. It works without a connection.

  • Stays on your device. Your pattern, ruler setting and choices are saved on the phone and nowhere else.
  • No account, no tracking. Nothing to sign up for, and the app has no analytics.
  • Free, with nothing to buy. No purchases and no ads.

For iPhone, iPad and Android. Your patterns stay yours, on your device.
